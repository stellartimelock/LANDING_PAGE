Privacy Policy

At Stellar TimeLock, we believe financial privacy is a fundamental right. This policy explains what (minimal) data our app interacts with and how it’s handled.

1. Non-Custodial Key Storage

Your secret seed and private keys are generated and stored exclusively on your device. They are protected by the Android Keystore (hardware-backed when available) or iOS Keychain with biometric authentication required for access. We never see, collect, store, or transmit your secret seeds, private keys, or recovery phrases. Your public key is sent to our backend solely to look up vault metadata you have created — it cannot be used to spend funds.

2. Google OAuth Usage

Google Sign-In is optional and used exclusively for restoring encrypted Google Drive backups. When you sign in, we receive your email address from Google and store it locally on your device to display alongside your backup status. Your email is never transmitted to our backend servers. The OAuth token is stored in Android Keystore / iOS Keychain and is only used to authenticate with Google Drive to read and write your encrypted backup envelope. We request only the drive.file and drive.appdata scopes — the minimum needed to manage backup files in your Drive’s app-specific storage. We never request access to your personal Drive files, contacts, or calendar data through this scope.

If you also opt into Google Calendar sync (for bill reminders), an additional calendar scope is requested at that time. This scope is also limited to reading and writing only the calendar events the app creates; your other calendar data is never accessed.

3. MongoDB Metadata

Our backend stores minimal metadata in MongoDB to support vault operations on the Stellar network. This includes your public key (a Stellar address starting with “G”), vault identifiers, lock/unlock timestamps, and on-chain status flags. No personally identifiable information, no email addresses, no IP addresses, and no device identifiers are stored. This metadata is necessary for the Soroban contract interaction layer to function — without it, the app cannot display your vault status correctly.

4. Google Drive Backup Encryption

When you enable Google Drive backup, your encrypted notebook data (notes, bills, ledger entries, TOTP secrets, and address book contacts) is bundled into an AES-256 encrypted envelope before upload. The encryption key is derived from your wallet seed via PBKDF2 and HKDF-SHA-256, meaning the backup is meaningless without your specific wallet’s secret material. Google Drive sees only an opaque encrypted blob — never plaintext. You can delete your backup at any time from within the app or directly from your Google Drive.

5. No Tracking

We do not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, or tracking tools. The app includes Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (Android only) solely for crash reporting and privacy-safe event counting (e.g., “wallet created,” “backup synced”). These events contain no personally identifiable information — no addresses, no amounts, no seeds, no emails. (Historical note: older “open-source analytics wrapper” links pointed at archived StellarTimeLock-web; see stellartimelock/WALLET for the current security reference.) which explicitly whitelists every allowed event. Your experience remains private and under your control.

6. User Data Control

You maintain full control over your data at all times:

  • Disconnecting your wallet wipes the seed and all encrypted data from this device.
  • You can export a manual JSON backup or use Google Drive backup at any time.
  • You can revoke Google Drive access from your Google Account settings at any time.
  • No account registration is required — the app works fully offline after download.
